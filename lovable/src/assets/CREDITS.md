# Earth textures

- `earth-surface.jpg` (2048 × 1024) and `earth-fast.jpg` (512 × 256): NASA GIBS layer `BlueMarble_ShadedRelief_Bathymetry`, the same layer the map tiles use, requested as one equirectangular image from the GIBS WMS (`https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/wms/epsg4326/best/wms.cgi`, EPSG:4326, full globe) and saved as JPEG. Imagery courtesy of NASA EOSDIS GIBS (https://www.earthdata.nasa.gov/gibs), part of EOSDIS.
